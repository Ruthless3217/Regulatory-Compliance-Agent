# Protection Benefits

## 1. Death Benefit

In case of unfortunate death of the Life Assured during the Policy Term, provided all due premiums have been paid and policy is in force, the Death Benefit payable will be:

1. Higher of Prevailing Sum Assured or Regular Premium Fund Value
2. Higher of Top-up Premium Fund Value or Top-up Premium Sum Assured
3. Sum Assured of Terminal Illness with Term Booster (if any)

Death Benefit shall not be less than 105% of Total Premiums Paid including Top-Up premiums.

## 2. Accidental Death Benefit (ADB)

In addition to Death Benefit, in case of accidental death of the Life Assured during the Policy Term, higher of ADB Sum Assured or Guaranteed Benefit will be paid.

## 3. Accidental Total Permanent Disability (ATPD)

If the Life Assured suffers Total Permanent Disability due to an accident, higher of ATPD Sum Assured or Guaranteed Benefit will be paid.

# Maturity Benefits

Fund Value plus the total premiums paid towards:
- Accidental Death Benefit
- Accidental Total Permanent Disability
- Terminal Illness with Term Booster
